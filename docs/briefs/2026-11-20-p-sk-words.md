# Brief P: Slovak word list repairs dropped háčky (ť, ľ, ď)

## Goal
Apple's text recognition often drops the háček of ť, ľ, ď ("robit", "velmi", "ludia"). Brief N removed the
letter-score guess because it changed correct words. This brief adds a word list instead: a word is repaired only
when its plain form is NOT a Slovak word (checked with the Slovak hunspell dictionary when the list is built) and
its háček form is a real, frequent Slovak word (Leipzig news corpus). Real plain words (byt, celkom, hotel, list,
let, mat, stat) are never in the list, so they never change.

## 1. Tool `tools/build-sk-words.mjs`
Header comment in the style of tools/build-letter-stats.mjs: what it builds, the two sources with licences, the
date (2026-11-20), usage `node tools/build-sk-words.mjs <words.txt> <sk_SK.aff> <sk_SK.dic>`.
1. Read the words file; for each line take the word, lowercase it, keep it only if it is letters only
   (`/^\p{L}+$/u`); sum counts per lowercase word.
2. `strip(w)`: ť→t, ľ→l, ď→d (only these three).
3. Candidates: words with at least one ť/ľ/ď, count ≥ 3 (named constant `MIN_COUNT`), that the dictionary accepts
   (`spell.correct(w)` or `spell.correct(w[0].toUpperCase() + w.slice(1))` for names like Ľubica).
4. Drop a candidate when ANY variant made by stripping a non-empty subset of its ť/ľ/ď letters is accepted by the
   dictionary (lowercase or capitalised): that plain form is a real word ("byt", "stal").
5. Group the remaining candidates by `strip(w)`. Keep the most frequent form; drop the whole group when the second
   form's count is at least a third of the first (ambiguous).
6. Write `packages/core/src/sk-words.ts`:
   ```ts
   // Generated by tools/build-sk-words.mjs; do not edit. <one line on what it is and the two sources + licences>
   export const SK_HACEK_WORDS = "<kept forms, most frequent first, separated by single spaces>";
   ```
   Print `forms <n> chars <length>` at the end.
7. Licence files: `licenses/leipzig-corpora-CC-BY-4.0.txt` (a short notice: "Slovak word frequencies from the
   Leipzig Corpora Collection, slk_newscrawl_2016_1M, Universität Leipzig. Licensed under CC BY 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Used to build a list of Slovak words with ť, ľ, ď.") and
   `licenses/hunspell-sk-MPL-2.0.txt` (the line "Slovak hunspell dictionary, sk-spell/hunspell-sk,
   https://github.com/sk-spell/hunspell-sk" then the full MPL-2.0 text from the curl above).
8. In `apps/mobile/scripts/gen-licenses.mjs` add two entries to `sources`, after PDF.js:

### Round 2 decision (orchestrator)
The dictionary lacks some real forms (adverb "ľahko", count 827). Rule 3 becomes: a candidate is accepted when the
dictionary accepts it (as above) OR its count ≥ 100 (named constant `CORPUS_ONLY_MIN`, comment: forms this common
in edited news text are real even when the dictionary lacks them). Rules 4 and 5 stay unchanged. Print also
`corpus-only <n>` (how many forms got in only by count).

### Round 3 decision (orchestrator, after running the repair over 180,000 correct news sentences)
False changes found: pod→poď (nspell rejects "pod"), ľuďom→ľudom, uvoľniť→uvolniť, McDonald's→McDonalďs,
d'Italia→ďItalia, Chmel→Chmeľ. Changes:
1. Tool, new rule 4b: also drop a candidate when any plain variant's corpus count ≥ `PLAIN_REAL_RATIO` × the
   candidate's count, with `PLAIN_REAL_RATIO = 0.5` (comment: a plain spelling that common is a real word or a
   name, e.g. "pod" 12790 vs "poď" 18; real dropped-háček typos are at most ~0.26, e.g. robit 734 vs robiť 2944).
2. scan.ts `fixDroppedHacek`: leave a word alone when it already contains ť, ľ or ď (any case).
3. scan.ts `APOSTROPHE_TDL`: after the apostrophe the letter must be lowercase (`\p{Ll}`, keep the whitespace/end/
   punctuation alternatives), and not an English possessive: not `s` followed by a non-letter or the end
   (McDonald's, Ted's). Rename `APOSTROPHE_PLAIN` to `PLAIN_LETTER` and declare it next to the other constants at
   the top.
4. New tests (unchanged input): "McDonald's", "d'Italia", "pod stolom", "Pod mostom", "ľuďom", "uvoľniť",
   "Chmel". All earlier tests still pass.

## 2. Repair rule in `packages/core/src/scan.ts`
Add to `fixSlovakLetters` (after the existing apostrophe rules, sk only) a step `fixDroppedHacek(text)`:
- A lazily built `Map<string, string>` from `SK_HACEK_WORDS.split(" ")`: key `strip(form)` → form (first wins).
  Build it on first use, not at import.
- Replace every `\p{L}+` word: `key = strip(word.toLowerCase())`; if the map has the key and the form differs from
  `word.toLowerCase()`, replace with the form in the word's case: all-uppercase word (length > 1) → form
  uppercased; first letter uppercase → form with first letter uppercased; else the form. Otherwise keep the word.
- Comment above it: why a list and not a guess (Brief N round 2), and that plain real words are excluded when the
  list is built.
